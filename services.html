Developmental Works & Schemes

Executing central and state welfare initiatives at the grassroots level

Ongoing Infrastructural Projects

  • CC Road and Interlocking Brick Pathways construction.
  • Installation and maintenance of solar-powered street lighting across main village paths.
  • Drainage network clean-up and expansion to prevent waterlogging.
  • Upgradation of primary village school facilities and Anganwadi centers.

Active Welfare Schemes

  • Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY): Providing permanent housing to eligible rural poor families.
  • MGNREGA: Job card registration and wage employment tracking for rural laborers.
  • Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin): Solid waste management and individual household toilet support.
  • Jal Jeevan Mission: Clean piped drinking water connection initiatives.
